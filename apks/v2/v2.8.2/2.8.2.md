# Toothy Clinic App - Version 2.8.2 Release Notes

This release resolves the native white screen bootstrap delay on slow emulators and devices (such as Bluestacks) by rendering a lightweight splash widget instantly upon boot.

## Key Changes
1. **Preloaded Splash UI**:
   * Defined `PreloadSplashWidget` at the bottom of [main.dart](file:///C:/BiboyStuffs/Flutterflow/toothy_c_o/lib/main.dart).
   * Called `runApp(const PreloadSplashWidget())` immediately after `WidgetsFlutterBinding.ensureInitialized()` in `main()`.
   * This immediately draws the purple splash screen, completely bypassing the native Android white container while Supabase initializes in the background.
   * Once `SupaFlow.initialize()` completes, the app executes `runApp(MyApp())` to transition cleanly into the main GoRouter layout.

2. **Staging Build**:
   * Packaged and staged the APK as `version 2.8.2.apk` inside `apks\v2.8.2\`.
